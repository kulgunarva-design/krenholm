A home. People. Memory.01 — 03
01 / HERITAGEFor future generations

More
than walls.

Cultural heritage is made of things that cannot be recreated: original details, human craftsmanship and the memory of a place.

Kreenholmi Inglise Meistrite Elamu is part of a history we feel responsible for. Its old brickwork and architectural details retain the work of skilled craftspeople. Caring for the house means respecting their work and treating what has survived with care.

Our aim is to preserve the character of the building and bring its history closer to people. Photographs, memories and family stories reveal the lives of different generations behind the façade. We want this connection with the past to endure for those who come after us.

01

Respect authenticity

Preserve the historic character of the house, respecting its original materials and the skill of its makers. Approach every change with care and consideration.

02

Preserve memories

Safeguard photographs, documents and memories. Behind every image are people and events that help us understand the history of the house and its surroundings.

03

Pass it on

Share the story of the house and explain why it matters to preserve it. So future generations can experience the original building and feel connected to their past.

03 / CONTACTThe story continues

Let’s stay
in touch.

People help keep the history of a place alive. If you have old photographs, documents or family memories connected with the house and its surroundings, we would be glad to hear about them. Even a small detail can add to our shared memory.
